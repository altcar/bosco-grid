The person behind the roll

I'm Shalev Lau. I study robotics at Bristol and co-founded METER, a navigation tool for blind and low-vision people. Before that I built VapeDrop. Most of what is on this page was taken on walks, buses and early mornings around Bristol, Cornwall and the road home.

The layout studies are the point: the same frames arranged nine different ways, still unfinished. If you want the proper version of my work, it lives at shalev.eu.org.
